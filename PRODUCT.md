# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), photos in `img/`, hosted on GitHub Pages. No frameworks, no tracking, system fonts.

## Users

- **Primary:** women in and around Manalapan, NJ looking for hair color (especially blondes), a cut, a blowout or a smoothing treatment. They arrive on a phone, usually from a Facebook group post or Instagram, inside an in-app browser. Many are drawn by the new-client special.
- **Also:** Abby, the owner, who gets this site for free and should see her own salon, done better than she imagined, on the first phone screen.
- Audience age: not stated (inferred general adult audience, not a 40+-specific one).

## Product Purpose

Replace a free Wix template whose "Book Now" buttons lead nowhere with a page that turns a visitor into a booking request by text, including after hours, while the new-client special is running.

## Positioning

A one-stylist, by-appointment salon in South Manalapan run by Abby, a stylist for 15 years, who specializes in hair color and high-lift blondes. Her sign-off: "Color. Style. Service by Abby".

## Operating Context

- Abby books by phone and text: "Call or text 908-839-6941" (Facebook ad, 2026-10-18).
- Hours: Monday–Friday, 8am–8pm, by appointment only (old site).
- Shows her work on Instagram @abbylivhair (verified live 2026-10-18: 112 posts, 495 followers).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services (verbatim, old site): "Specializing in hair color and high lift blondes"; "keratin treatments, smoothing systems by GK, and hair cuts along with a perfectly styled blow dry".
- Prices (old site): Blow dry & styling $45+; Cutting $75+; Highlights/balayage: Face frame $70, Partial $100+, Full $250+; Single process $60; Glaze $40. Keratin / GK smoothing: no published price.
- New-client special (Facebook ad, 2026-10-18, "Limited Time!"): Root Touch Up $40; Haircut & Blow Dry $60; Highlights starting at $50; Blowouts only $30.
- Area: South Manalapan, NJ. **No street address may be published** (unknown; possibly home-based).
- No email, no Facebook business page, no Google listing, no reviews. None may be invented or implied.
- Booking: text message hand-off (`sms:`) composed from a short request form; calling via the header number.

## Brand Commitments

- Name exactly as written: "Abbyliv hair".
- Logo: brush-script "AL" monogram with "ABBYLIV HAIR" on blush pink (Instagram avatar, 100px only).
- Tone: warm, pampering: "Treat Yourself", "Take time to pamper yourself", "look—and feel—like a million dollars".
- Voice: first person, "I / my" (solo stylist; inferred from "by Abby").

## Evidence on Hand

- Six work photos from her own Wix site, backs of heads, no faces (`img/work-01..06.webp`; originals in ignored `src-assets/`). One blonde high-lift, one copper smooth, four brunette blowouts/curls.
- Logo avatar from Instagram (`img/logo.webp`).
- Absent: before/after pairs, reviews, portrait of Abby, salon address, email.

## Product Principles

1. Phone first: the first screen says who, what, and offers booking.
2. Honest: only what Abby has published; gaps go to the handoff, not the page.
3. The special is the hook; booking by text is the fix.
4. Speak as Abby, about how she treats each client.
